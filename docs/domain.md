# Domain notes

## Business flow

1. **Catalog** — staff maintain products and categories.
2. **Farm** — plots, crop batches, activities, harvest events add stock.
3. **Sell** — orders deduct stock; payments reduce công nợ.
4. **Reports** — revenue, top products, yield vs sold, receivables, profit.
